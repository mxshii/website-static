our story

a pen & a dream.

STATIC started the same way most good things do — out of boredom and too much coffee. We wanted stickers that felt warm, personal, and a little weird. So we started drawing.

Every design is hand-illustrated, printed on premium waterproof vinyl, and shipped from Egypt with love. No mass production, no AI art — just us, drawing tiny things for you to stick on big stuff.
