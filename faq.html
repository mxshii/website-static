frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our waterproof stickers, Alexandria delivery, and payments.

Are the stickers really waterproof?

Yes! They are printed on waterproof vinyl with an extra protective matte film. You can put them on water bottles, hand-wash them, or expose them to outdoor weather without smudging or peeling.

Where do you deliver and how long does it take?

Currently, we deliver exclusively across Alexandria! Orders arrive in 1–2 business days for a flat rate of 50 EGP.

How do I pay with Vodafone Cash?

During checkout, select Vodafone Cash. Send your order total to 01005792211. We verify and process your order immediately!

What is your returns / exchange policy?

If your stickers arrive damaged or defective, reach out to us via the Contact page with your Order ID, and we'll gladly replace them or refund you.

What sizes are the stickers?

Single die-cut stickers range from 5cm to 8cm. Sticker sheets are standard A5 size featuring multiple contour-cut peelable designs.
