Our Story

How a cup of coffee, a sketchbook, and an obsession with stickers turned into STATIC.

the origin

just a cat,
a pen & a dream.

STATIC was born in Cairo out of a desire to make things you can actually hold, touch, and stick on your favorite stuff. We got tired of generic mass-produced stickers that peel off after two days or fade in the sun.

Every single sticker starts as a rough hand drawing on paper before being digitally inked, colored, and printed onto heavy-duty waterproof vinyl with scratch-resistant matte coating.

"we draw it, you stick it."

100% Hand-Drawn

No AI templates, no clip art. Every stroke is crafted with passion by independent artists.

Weatherproof Vinyl

Durable 100-micron vinyl with UV laminate. Slap them on hydroflasks, laptops, or car bumpers.

Carefully Packaged

Every order comes in signature beige kraft mailers with surprise freebie mini-stickers inside.

frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our stickers & orders

Are the stickers really waterproof?

Yes! They are printed on waterproof vinyl with an extra protective matte film. You can put them on water bottles, hand-wash them, or expose them to outdoor weather without smudging or peeling.

How long does delivery take in Egypt?

Cairo and Giza delivery usually takes 2–3 business days. Alexandria, Delta, and other Governorates take 3–4 business days.

How do I pay with Vodafone Cash?

During checkout, select Vodafone Cash. Send your order total to 01005792211. We will verify and process your order immediately!

What is your returns / exchange policy?

If your stickers arrive damaged or defective, reach out to us via the Contact page with your Order ID, and we'll gladly replace them or refund you.
